# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## The addition that matters here

This project publishes measurements about other people's work. Two rules follow
from that, and they are not optional:

**Criticise measurements, never maintainers.** A tool that does not do what it
claims is a finding about the tool. It is not evidence of dishonesty,
incompetence, or bad faith on the part of anyone who built it. Most overstated
claims come from measuring the wrong thing, which is easy — this project exists
because it is easy, and its own author's tool is the first row on the board.

**No dunking.** Not in issues, not in commit messages, not in posts linking
here. A verdict is a number and a method. If a finding cannot be stated without
a jab, state it without the jab.

Maintainers of audited tools arriving to dispute a row are contributors, and
are to be treated as the most valuable kind.

## Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Focusing on what is measurable, and saying plainly when something is not
- Accepting a correction to a published row without defensiveness
- Showing empathy toward other community members
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Presenting a weakly-supported number as a strong one to win an argument
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the reporter
will be respected.
